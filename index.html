Your Options Explained

4 Ways to Avoid Foreclosure

Understanding your options is the first step. Here's an honest look at each path — the pros and the cons.

1

Sell on the MLS (Traditional Sale)

List your home with a real estate agent on the open market for maximum exposure to potential buyers.

Pros

  • Maximum exposure
  • Potential top dollar

Cons

  • Agent commissions & costs
  • Takes time (60-90+ days)
  • Repairs often required
2

Work with Your Bank (Loan Modification)

Contact your lender to restructure or modify your existing loan terms to make payments more manageable.

Pros

  • Keep your home
  • Lower monthly payments

Cons

  • Expensive process
  • No guarantees of approval
  • Can take months
3

Short Sale (with Free Attorney)

Sell for less than what's owed with bank approval. We provide a short sale attorney at no cost to you.

Pros

  • Attorney at no cost
  • Avoid foreclosure on record
  • Less credit damage

Cons

  • Time consuming process
  • No guarantee bank approves
4

Sell to Our Investment Group

Get a fair cash offer and close on your timeline — as fast as 7 days. No repairs, no fees, no hassle.

Pros

  • Quick close (7-14 days)
  • Cash offer, no financing
  • Sell as-is, no repairs
  • No commissions or fees

Cons

  • Below full market value
