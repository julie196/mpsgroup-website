# Illinois Real Estate Licensee Website Disclosure Requirements

## Key Findings from 225 ILCS 454/10-30 (Real Estate License Act of 2000)

### Section 10-30(c) - Disclosure of Licensee Status:
> "A licensee shall disclose, in writing, to all parties in a transaction the licensee's status as a licensee and any and all interest the licensee has or may have in the real estate constituting the subject matter thereof."

### Section 10-30(c)(2)(B) - Purchasing/Leasing Advertisements:
> "If a sponsored or inactive licensee runs advertisements, for the purpose of purchasing or leasing real estate, the licensee shall disclose in the advertisements the licensee's status as a licensee."

**This is the KEY provision for Douglas.** Since MPS Group Inc is advertising to PURCHASE properties from homeowners, and Douglas is a licensed agent, he MUST disclose his status as a licensee in the advertisements.

### Section 10-30(d) - Sponsoring Broker Name Required:
> "Advertising in any media shall be under the direct supervision of the sponsoring or designated managing broker and in the sponsoring broker's business name, which in the case of a franchise shall include the franchise affiliation as well as the name of the individual firm."

**EXCEPTION (Section 10-30(d)(2)):** This does NOT apply "when a licensee is selling or leasing real estate owned by the licensee or buying or leasing real estate for their own use."

### Section 10-30(e):
> "No licensee shall list the licensee's name or otherwise advertise in the licensee's own name to the general public through any medium of advertising as being in the real estate business without listing the sponsoring broker's business name."

### Section 10-30(f):
> "The sponsoring broker's business name and the name of the licensee must appear in all advertisements, including business cards."

## Administrative Code Section 1450.715(b):
> "All advertising must include the sponsoring broker's name"

---

## ANALYSIS FOR DOUGLAS MAY / MPS GROUP INC:

### What Douglas MUST disclose:
1. **His status as a licensed real estate agent** - Required under 10-30(c)(2)(B) since he's advertising to PURCHASE properties
2. **His sponsoring broker's name (Epique Realty)** - Required under 10-30(d), (e), and (f) UNLESS the exception in 10-30(d)(2) applies

### Does the exception in 10-30(d)(2) apply?
The exception says the sponsoring broker name requirement doesn't apply "when a licensee is buying or leasing real estate for their own use." 

If Douglas/MPS Group is buying properties as an investor (for their own use/portfolio), the sponsoring broker name MAY not be required. However, this is a gray area — "for their own use" could be interpreted narrowly.

### RECOMMENDATION:
To be safe and compliant, Douglas should include:
1. A disclosure that he is a licensed real estate agent in Illinois
2. His sponsoring broker's name (Epique Realty) — even if the exception might apply, it's safer to include it

### Suggested footer disclosure:
"Douglas May is a licensed real estate agent in the State of Illinois, sponsored by Epique Realty."

OR more minimal (if relying on the exception):
"Douglas May is a licensed real estate agent in the State of Illinois."
